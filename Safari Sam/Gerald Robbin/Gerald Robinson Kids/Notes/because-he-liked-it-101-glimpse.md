## What the book shows overall

- **The book leans toward "the oddest creature wins."** About 45 pages cover the ocean or deep sea. Bats alone get 11 pages, and dogs, cats and elephants each get several.
- **Four kinds of "why" run through it:** a body part with an odd purpose (cow teeth, cube poop), a record (blue whale, snailfish), a survival trick (sea slug, polar bear, opossum), and "smarter than you think" (crow, bee, pigeon, dog).
- **The caption jokes are a second layer.** Examples are the sloth's "his breath will take you out" and the beaver's HOA line.
- **Repeat topics are deliberate.** Dogs run pages 1–3 and 54–55, crows 17 and 53, and the Kitti's bat 8 and 95. The fruit fly, fish-cough and goldfish pages in the 80s and 90s deliver the "smarter than you think" theme.
