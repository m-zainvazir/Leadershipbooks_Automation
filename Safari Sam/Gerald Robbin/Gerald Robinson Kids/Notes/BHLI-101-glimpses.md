I read all 101 pages of *Because He Liked It! In the Animal World*, using the OCR text layer because the PDF is too big to open directly. Each page follows one pattern: a "Why did God do that?" header, a short fact about an animal, and a joke caption from the animal in a speech bubble. The answer is always "Because He liked it!"

The numbers below are the printed page numbers. Creatures that appear on more than one page are grouped under one heading. Each entry gives the page, the implied question, and the answer the page gives.

## 🐾 Land: pets and farm animals

## What the book shows overall

- **The book leans toward "the oddest creature wins."** About 45 pages cover the ocean or deep sea. Bats alone get 11 pages, and dogs, cats and elephants each get several.
- **Four kinds of "why" run through it:** a body part with an odd purpose (cow teeth, cube poop), a record (blue whale, snailfish), a survival trick (sea slug, polar bear, opossum), and "smarter than you think" (crow, bee, pigeon, dog).
- **The caption jokes are a second layer.** Examples are the sloth's "his breath will take you out" and the beaver's HOA line.
- **Repeat topics are deliberate.** Dogs run pages 1–3 and 54–55, crows 17 and 53, and the Kitti's bat 8 and 95. The fruit fly, fish-cough and goldfish pages in the 80s and 90s deliver the "smarter than you think" theme.

## Things you may want to check

1. **Unnamed animals.** Pages 42 and 72 don't name the frog. Page 84's fish is my guess from scrambled OCR.
2. **Fact checks.**
   - Manatee "special bladders" (p11) is shaky. The usual explanation is dense bones plus intestinal gas and lung control.
   - Sloths not farting (p14) is disputed.
   - Page 8 calls the bumblebee bat the "smallest mammal," while the Etruscan shrew is a rival for that title.
