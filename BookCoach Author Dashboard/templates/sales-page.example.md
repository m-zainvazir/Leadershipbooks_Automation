## The Coach Starts With You

Before telling you what you should do, your Publishing Coach needs to understand what you’re trying to accomplish.

Why are you writing this book?

Who is it for?

What do you want the book to accomplish?

Is your goal to sell books—or is the book intended to build authority, generate business, support a speaking career, preserve a story, establish expertise, entertain readers, or accomplish something entirely different?

Do you actually know who your reader is?

Is the book you’re planning the right book for that reader?

Should you even be thinking about publishing yet?

Those questions matter.

And they can completely change the decisions that follow.

Your coach isn’t there simply to agree with you. It’s there to help you think like an experienced publisher.
